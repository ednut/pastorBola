# Pastor Bola Akinlabi — website concept

An editorial ministry home built from the supplied profile plan and public ministry research.

## Visual identity

- Deep plum: #432D3B — primary brand colour and calls to action.
- Oxblood accent: #754657 — italic emphasis and warm accents.
- Warm ivory: #F7F3ED — primary background.
- Soft parchment: #EDE5D9 — alternate sections.
- Muted gold: #AD9063 — fine lines and small decorative details.
- Headings: Bodoni Moda, with selective italic emphasis.
- Body and navigation: DM Sans.

This is a proposed identity, not a claim about an existing approved brand. It responds to the official red silk-and-pearl portrait and the warm, confident tone in the supplied plan.

## Pages

Home; Her story; Ministry; Books & resources; Watch & listen; Speaking; Contact; Privacy.

The layout draws on the references' editorial hierarchy, portrait-led composition, personal storytelling and clear routes into teaching and books. No purchased template or copied template source was used.

## Current behaviour

- Responsive desktop and mobile navigation.
- Media filters for teaching and relationship conversations.
- Links to real teaching and the Better Half conversation.
- Speaking invitation builder validates fields, prepares a draft, and allows copying.
- Newsletter is a clearly labelled design preview. It sends and stores no email addresses.
- Speaking invitations are not delivered automatically. The draft links to Pastor Bola's official Instagram so visitors can request her office's booking channel.

## Before official launch

Connect the approved mailing-list service and office enquiry destination. Confirm book titles, covers, dates and sales links. Review all biography and ministry copy with her team. Confirm permission to reuse the source photographs. Add the final privacy notice, custom domain and any analytics required.
